## InVideo AI Agent One — 마스터 프롬프트 (v2 고퀄 버전)

> InVideo Agent One 접속 → 새 프로젝트 → 아래 프롬프트 전체 복붙

```
Create an ultra-premium luxury perfume commercial for global brand advertising.

═══════════════════════════════════
PRODUCT DETAILS
═══════════════════════════════════
Bottle: Tall rectangular dark amber glass bottle — internal golden liquid visible through glass
with natural light scattering and refraction effects. Brushed titanium cap with micro-engraved
geometric pattern, mirror-polished edge. Minimalist embossed logo on frosted glass label panel.
Surface: Hand-polished obsidian black marble — deep mirror reflection below the bottle,
micro-grain texture catching light at oblique angles.

═══════════════════════════════════
SCENE PROGRESSION — 10 SHOTS
═══════════════════════════════════

SHOT 1 — VELVET REVEAL SETUP
Midnight blue velvet cloth draped fully over bottle. Single overhead spotlight creates
a dramatic cone of light through the fabric. Deep shadow surrounds the form.
Camera: Static wide. Hold 3 seconds.

SHOT 2 — SILK CLOTH REMOVAL
Velvet cloth slowly pulled back from above — bottle gradually emerges into light.
Rim light traces the bottle silhouette as it appears. Soft fill light from lower right.
Camera: Slow dolly push-in as cloth reveals. 0.5x slow motion.

SHOT 3 — LOGO MACRO
Extreme close-up on embossed logo. Shallow depth of field — letters razor-sharp,
background falls into warm bokeh. Micro surface grain and light engraving detail visible.
Camera: Static macro. Rack focus from background to logo.

SHOT 4 — FRONT HERO SHOT
Full bottle centered on marble. Three-point studio lighting:
— Key light: large soft octabox from upper-left, soft shadow gradient across marble
— Fill light: V-flat white reflector from right, reducing harsh shadows by 50%
— Rim light: thin LED strip behind bottle, silver-gold halo effect
Camera: Slow dolly push-in. Eye-level. Hold 3 seconds.

SHOT 5 — SIDE PROFILE SILHOUETTE
Bottle angled 90°. Single backlight only — bottle becomes a glowing amber silhouette
against pure black. Internal liquid luminosity effect — golden light glows from within.
Camera: Slow orbital arc from front to pure side. 0.7x slow motion.

SHOT 6 — GLASS TEXTURE MACRO
Macro shot of glass surface showing:
— Light caustics (refracted light patterns) dancing across the marble
— Internal amber liquid with visible suspended micro-particles
— Condensation micro-droplets on glass exterior catching rim light
Camera: Extreme close-up. Very shallow DOF. Soft rack focus through glass layers.

SHOT 7 — CAP DETAIL
Titanium cap fills the frame. Mirror polish reflects a soft studio light blob.
Micro-engraving pattern catches the key light at precise angles.
Brushed texture alternating with polished strips — fine material luxury.
Camera: Static macro. Single point light source. High contrast.

SHOT 8 — FRAGRANCE MIST
Fine golden mist sprays from the bottle nozzle in ultra slow motion (0.2x).
Mie scattering effect — particles glow amber-gold where they catch the backlight.
Particles drift and curl naturally in the air.
Subtle volumetric light beam visible through the mist cloud.
Camera: Side angle. Deep black background. 0.2x ultra slow motion.

SHOT 9 — OVERHEAD COMPOSITION
Top-down aerial shot. Bottle centered on marble.
Dried dark rose petals scattered naturally to left and lower-right.
Thin layer of gold dust near the petals. Mirror reflection perfectly symmetrical below.
Camera: Directly overhead. Static. Slow zoom out.

SHOT 10 — CLOSING CARD
Pure black frame. Brand handle or logo fades in center — clean white type.
Hold 2 seconds. Slow fade to black.
Camera: None. Text only on black.

═══════════════════════════════════
LIGHTING SYSTEM
═══════════════════════════════════
Overall mood: Dark luxury — deep shadow with precision light
Color temperature: Warm key light (3200K) + cool rim light (6500K) = premium contrast
Shadow: Crushed blacks — no detail in shadow areas beyond 15% brightness
Highlights: Specular highlights clipped to pure white on metal surfaces only
Atmosphere: Subtle smoke haze layer at 10% opacity for depth and light beam visibility

═══════════════════════════════════
CAMERA & MOTION
═══════════════════════════════════
Lens: 85mm–105mm equivalent (flattering compression, minimal distortion)
Aperture: f/1.4–f/2.8 (extreme bokeh on background)
Motion style: All movements imperceptibly slow — no jarring cuts
Color grade: Deep crushed blacks / warm amber highlights / slight cool-blue midtone tint
Film grain: Fine 35mm grain overlay at 15% opacity

═══════════════════════════════════
OUTPUT SPECIFICATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════
Orientation: Portrait 1080×1920px (9:16 Instagram Reels)
Pacing: Each shot minimum 2.5 seconds — let each frame breathe
Style reference: Dior Sauvage / YSL Black Opium global campaign level
Final result: Indistinguishable from a multi-million dollar fragrance commercial
```
